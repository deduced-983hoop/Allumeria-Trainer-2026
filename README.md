## 🎮 About the Game

Allumeria is a voxel sandbox RPG developed by Infinimelon and released in Early Access on August 28, 2026. It combines exploration, survival crafting, open-world building, dungeon crawling, boss battles, and progression through weapons, abilities, resources, and trinkets.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Allumeria**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and progression enhancements for Allumeria. It can protect the player during exploration and combat, simplify resource collection and crafting, improve movement abilities, and support faster access to weapons, trinkets, dungeons, and other progression systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Allumeria's voxel building, exploration, crafting, dungeons, bosses, weapons, trinkets, and procedural worlds with fewer progression and resource limitations.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents the player from taking damage while exploring caves, dungeons, biomes, and hostile nighttime environments.
- **One-Hit Harvesting** — Speeds up gathering from trees, stone, coal, ore deposits, and other breakable world resources.
- **Easy Crafting** — Reduces or removes material requirements for crafting tools, workbenches, furnaces, weapons, building materials, and other items.
- **Free Trinket Effects** — Keeps selected movement and utility trinket effects active without normal restrictions.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Health** — Keeps the player's health at its maximum value during encounters with enemies and bosses.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Increases outgoing damage for swords, crossbows, bombs, special weapons, and other combat items.
- **Rapid Attacks** — Reduces attack delays to make weapons and offensive abilities respond more quickly.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Items** — Prevents selected inventory resources, materials, ammunition, and consumables from being depleted.
- **Instant Furnace Processing** — Speeds up the conversion of mined ore into ingots at furnaces.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Movement Boost** — Enhances movement speed and supports easier traversal when using sprinting, jumping, climbing, gliding, or flying abilities.
- **No Fall Damage** — Protects the player from damage caused by drops while exploring caves, towers, floating islands, and elevated structures.

### 📌 Building

- **Unlimited Building Materials** — Allows construction projects to continue without consuming selected blocks and building resources.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate movement features before traveling through caves, dungeons, floating areas, or dangerous terrain. Movement boosts are useful for reaching elevated structures and combining sprinting, jumping, climbing, gliding, or flying trinket abilities. Disable enhanced movement near narrow platforms and while building to avoid accidental falls or misplaced blocks.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory with E and select the desired items, tools, materials, or consumables before enabling inventory features. Unlimited Items preserves selected quantities during crafting and use, while Easy Crafting helps create workbenches, tools, weapons, furnaces, building blocks, and other equipment with fewer material requirements. Save the world after major inventory changes.

### World & Environment

Use protection and gathering options when exploring procedurally generated worlds, forests, caves, and other biomes. One-Hit Harvesting accelerates collection of wood, flint, stone, coal, copper, and other resources. Unlimited Building Materials is intended for large shelters, towns, lighting projects, shaped blocks, and painted structures. Save regularly because world content and building systems may change during Early Access.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable God Mode or Unlimited Health before entering dungeons or challenging bosses. Damage Multiplier and Rapid Attacks work with the game's varied arsenal, including swords, crossbows, bombs, shock arrows, boomerangs, shuriken launchers, and other special weapons. For normal progression, use combat options selectively so boss encounters and ability unlocks remain meaningful.
